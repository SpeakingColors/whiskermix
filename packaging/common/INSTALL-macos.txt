WhiskerMix for macOS
====================
1. Open WhiskerMix-macOS.pkg. If macOS blocks it (not notarized), right-click the .pkg, choose Open, then Open again.
2. The installer puts WhiskerMix.app and "Uninstall WhiskerMix.app" in Applications, the Shimmer plugins in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3, and the manual in /Library/Application Support/Studio 87/WhiskerMix.
3. Start WhiskerMix and choose your audio device in Settings.
Note: macOS builds mix audio only. Digiface USB hardware control is Linux only for now.
Uninstall: open "Uninstall WhiskerMix" from Applications (asks whether to keep your workspaces and snapshots).
