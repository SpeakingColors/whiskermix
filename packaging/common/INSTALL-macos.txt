WhiskerMix for macOS
====================
1. Unzip, drag WhiskerMix.app to Applications.
2. First launch: right-click the app, choose Open (the app is not notarized yet).
3. Effect plugins: copy the .vst3 files from "plugins" to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
Note: macOS builds mix audio only. Digiface USB control (hardware mixer) is Linux only for now.
Full guide: manual/MANUAL.md
