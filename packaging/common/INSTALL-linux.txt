WhiskerMix for Linux
====================
1. Unzip anywhere, open a terminal in the folder.
2. Run:  sh install.sh
   This copies the app to ~/.local/opt/whiskermix, the effect plugins (VST3) to ~/.vst3,
   adds a menu entry, and installs the Digiface USB rule (asks for sudo once).
3. Unplug and replug the Digiface USB, then start "WhiskerMix" from your menu.
Needs PipeWire (default on Bazzite, Fedora, Ubuntu 24.04+) and libusb-1.0 (installed by default on most systems).
Full guide: manual/MANUAL.md. Uninstall: sh uninstall.sh
